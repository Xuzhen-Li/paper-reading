# Density and dedup

导读 uses the shortest sufficient chain:

`core observation → decisive comparison or method evidence → main uncertainty → bound that would change the conclusion`

Do not recap the lab diary unless order itself is evidence.

## Paragraph test

If removing the paragraph leaves the central claim intact **and** the information exists elsewhere, delete or merge.

## Claim functions

The same finding may appear at most three times, each with a different job:

| Place | Job |
|-------|-----|
| 速览卡 | introduce（作者；有全套脚本则代码） |
| 全文导读 | demonstrate (with figure/number) |
| 我的判断 | interpret (cite / do not treat as) |

数字速查 stores quantities. It must not restate the one-liner.

Do not hide an inconvenient result or a bound to look tighter. Extra numbers from SI tables go to 数字速查. The SI files themselves live under `FIGURES_DIR/<slug>/si/`, not as a second appendix in the note.
